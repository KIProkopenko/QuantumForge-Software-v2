# Freedom Coalition

## Overview
The Freedom Coalition is a resistance movement fighting to restore democracy and freedom to the galaxy. Opposed to the Dominion Authority's tyranny.

## Major Operations
- Stealing the Void Core plans
- Battle of the Void Core
- Battle of Cryoplan
- Battle of Verdantis
- Assault on Nexus Prime

## Victory
The Freedom Coalition triumphed when Kael Torren redeemed Xarn Velgor and destroyed Lord Malachar.
