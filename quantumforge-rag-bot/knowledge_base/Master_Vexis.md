# Master Vexis

## Overview
Master Vexis is a legendary Flux Guardian and the grandmaster of the Order of Wardens. Despite his small stature, he is one of the most powerful Flux-sensitives in history.

## Background
Master Vexis has trained Flux Guardians for centuries, with over 900 years of wisdom.

## Notable Quotes
"Do or do not. There is no try."
