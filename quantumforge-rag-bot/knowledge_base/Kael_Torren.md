# Kael Torren

## Overview
Kael Torren is a Flux-sensitive hero who rose from a humble background on Aridus Prime to become one of the most powerful Flux Guardians in history. He is the son of Xarn Velgor.

## Early Life
Born on Aridus Prime, Kael grew up as an orphan, unaware of his true parentage. He dreamed of becoming a pilot. His destiny changed when he discovered Unit-7, carrying a message from Orin Kestrel.

## Journey
- Discovered his Flux-sensitivity under Orin Kestrel's guidance
- Joined the Freedom Coalition
- Trained as a Flux Guardian
- Confronted his father, Xarn Velgor
- Redeemed his father and destroyed Lord Malachar

## Abilities
- Exceptional pilot and marksman
- Powerful connection to the Radiant Path
- Skilled Plasma Blade wielder
- Natural leader
