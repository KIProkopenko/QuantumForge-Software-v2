# Interceptor-7

## Overview
The Interceptor-7, also known as the X-Wing starfighter, is the primary starfighter of the Freedom Coalition.

## Specifications
- Manufacturer: Incom Corporation
- Length: 12.5 meters
- Crew: 1 pilot + 1 astromech droid
- Armament: 4 laser cannons, 2 proton torpedo launchers
