# Lyra Solari

## Overview
Lyra Solari is the leader of the Freedom Coalition and a key figure in the resistance against the Dominion Authority. A former royal princess of Alderis Prime, she became a symbol of hope after her homeworld was destroyed by the Void Core.

## Background
Lyra was raised as a princess on Alderis Prime, trained in diplomacy and leadership. When the Dominion Authority destroyed her homeworld, she joined the Freedom Coalition and quickly rose to leadership due to her courage and strategic mind.

## Role in the Resistance
- Organized the theft of the Void Core schematics
- Led the assault on the first Void Core
- Coordinated guerrilla operations across the galaxy
- Negotiated alliances with neutral star systems

## Skills
- Expert tactician and strategist
- Skilled diplomat and negotiator
- Proficient with blaster pistols
- Strong connection to the Radiant Path
