# Interceptor-7

## Overview
The Interceptor-7, also known as the X-Wing starfighter, is the primary starfighter of the Freedom Coalition. Fast, agile, and heavily armed, it is the workhorse of the Rebel fleet.

## Specifications
- Manufacturer: Incom Corporation
- Length: 12.5 meters
- Crew: 1 pilot + 1 astromech droid
- Armament: 4 laser cannons, 2 proton torpedo launchers
- Shields: Deflector shields
- Hyperdrive: Class 2.0

## Design Features
- S-foils (wings) that adjust for atmosphere/space
- R2-series astromech droid socket
- Ejection seat
- Advanced targeting systems

## Notable Squadrons
- **Red Squadron**: Led by Kael Torren in the Battle of the Void Core
- **Rogue Squadron**: Elite pilots led by Wedge Antilles
- **Green Squadron**: Reconnaissance and patrol

## The Battle of the Void Core
Interceptor-7s played a crucial role in the attack on the Void Core. Despite heavy losses, Kael Torren successfully exploited the thermal exhaust port weakness, destroying the superweapon.

## Variants
- T-65B: Standard model
- T-65C-A2: Enhanced shielding
- T-65XJ3: Experimental long-range

## Legacy
The Interceptor-7 became a symbol of hope and resistance. Its distinctive shape and red stripes are instantly recognizable across the galaxy.
