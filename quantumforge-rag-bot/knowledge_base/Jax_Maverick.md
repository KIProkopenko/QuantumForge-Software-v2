# Jax Maverick

## Overview
Jax Maverick is a rogue pilot, smuggler, and reluctant hero who became a key figure in the Freedom Coalition.

## The Star Runner
Jax's ship, the Star Runner, is a modified YT-1300 freighter known for its speed.

## Role in the War
- Transported Kael Torren across the galaxy
- Participated in the Battle of the Void Core
- Led smuggling operations
