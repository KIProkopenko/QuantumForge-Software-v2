# Orin Kestrel

## Overview
Orin Kestrel is a legendary Flux Guardian and mentor to Kael Torren. One of the last surviving members of the Order of Wardens.

## Background
Orin was a respected Flux Guardian who fought in the Clone Wars. After the rise of the Dominion Authority, he went into hiding on Aridus Prime.

## Legacy
Orin Kestrel's wisdom and sacrifice inspired a new generation of Flux Wardens.
