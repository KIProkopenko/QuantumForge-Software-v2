# Synth Flux

## Overview
The Synth Flux is a metaphysical and ubiquitous energy field that binds the galaxy together. Generated by all living things.

## The Two Paths
- **Radiant Path**: Compassion, healing, defense, knowledge
- **Void Path**: Fear, anger, hatred, aggression

## Abilities
- Telekinesis
- Mind tricks
- Precognition
- Enhanced reflexes
