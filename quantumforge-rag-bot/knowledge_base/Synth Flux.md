# Synth Flux

## Overview
The Synth Flux is a metaphysical and ubiquitous energy field that binds the galaxy together. Generated by all living things, it can be manipulated by those who are Flux-sensitive.

## Nature of the Flux
- Created by all living organisms
- Surrounds and penetrates all things
- Binds the galaxy together
- Can be felt and manipulated by the sensitive

## The Two Paths
The Flux has two aspects:
- **Radiant Path**: Compassion, healing, defense, knowledge
- **Void Path**: Fear, anger, hatred, aggression

## Flux-Sensitivity
Approximately 1 in 50,000 beings are born with the ability to sense and manipulate the Flux. This ability is determined by midi-chlorians, microscopic life forms that symbiotically exist in all living cells.

## Abilities
- Telekinesis (moving objects)
- Mind tricks (influencing the weak-minded)
- Precognition (sensing the future)
- Enhanced reflexes and agility
- Healing (Radiant Path)
- Force lightning (Void Path)

## The Prophecy
An ancient prophecy speaks of a "Chosen One" who will bring balance to the Flux by destroying the Void Disciples. This prophecy was believed to be fulfilled by Xarn Velgor, but ultimately by his son, Kael Torren.

## Flux Echoes
Powerful Flux-sensitives can preserve their consciousness after death, becoming Flux Echoes. They can appear as spectral beings and guide the living.

## Legacy
The Synth Flux is the foundation of the Flux Warden tradition. Understanding and mastering it is the path to becoming a true guardian of peace and justice.
