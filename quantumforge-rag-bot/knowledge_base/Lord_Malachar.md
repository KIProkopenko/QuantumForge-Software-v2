# Lord Malachar

## Overview
Lord Malachar is the Emperor of the Dominion Authority and the master of Xarn Velgor. A master of the Void Path.

## Rise to Power
- Manipulated the Galactic Senate for decades
- Orchestrated the Clone Wars
- Executed Order 66
- Declared himself Emperor

## Downfall
Lord Malachar was ultimately defeated when Xarn Velgor redeemed himself.
