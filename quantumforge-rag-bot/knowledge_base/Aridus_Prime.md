# Aridus Prime

## Overview
Aridus Prime is a harsh desert planet located in the Outer Rim Territories. Known for its twin suns, endless dunes, and moisture farming communities.

## Geography
- Terrain: Endless desert with rocky canyons
- Climate: Extremely hot and arid
- Notable locations: Dusthaven, Obi-Wan's Cave

## Role in the War
Aridus Prime served as the starting point for Kael Torren's journey.
