# Plasma Blade

## Overview
The Plasma Blade is an elegant weapon for a more civilized age. An energy sword powered by a kyber crystal, it is the signature weapon of Flux Wardens and Void Disciples.

## Construction
- Hilt: Customized to the wielder
- Blade: Plasma loop contained by magnetic field
- Power source: Kyber crystal
- Colors: Blue, green (Wardens), red (Void Disciples)

## The Kyber Crystal
Kyber crystals are Force-sensitive crystals that resonate with the Flux. They are found on planets like Ilum and Mustafar Prime. A crystal "calls" to its wielder.

## Colors and Meaning
- **Blue**: Flux Guardians (combat-focused)
- **Green**: Flux Consulars (wisdom-focused)
- **Red**: Void Disciples (created by "bleeding" a crystal with the Void Path)
- **Yellow**: Flux Sentinels (balance of combat and wisdom)
- **Purple**: Rare, balance of light and dark
- **White**: Purified red crystals

## Combat Forms
There are seven forms of Plasma Blade combat:
1. Form I: Shii-Cho (basic)
2. Form II: Makashi (dueling)
3. Form III: Soresu (defense)
4. Form IV: Ataru (acrobatic)
5. Form V: Djem So (power)
6. Form VI: Niman (balanced)
7. Form VII: Juyo/Vaapad (aggressive)

## Notable Plasma Blades
- **Orin Kestrel's blade**: Blue, passed to Kael Torren
- **Xarn Velgor's blade**: Red, symbol of his fall
- **Master Vexis's blade**: Green, small hilt for his size
- **Kael Torren's blade**: Green, later blue when reconstructed

## Legacy
The Plasma Blade represents the discipline and wisdom of the Flux Wardens. Despite the purge, the tradition lives on through those who wield it with honor.
