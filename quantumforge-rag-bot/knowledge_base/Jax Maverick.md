# Jax Maverick

## Overview
Jax Maverick is a rogue pilot, smuggler, and reluctant hero who became a key figure in the Freedom Coalition. Known for his sharp wit, quick reflexes, and his ship, the Star Runner, Jax represents the everyman who rises to greatness.

## Background
Originally from the industrial world of Corellia Secundus, Jax learned to fly before he could walk. He became a smuggler to escape his debts, transporting cargo for various criminal organizations, including Zorak the Crime Lord.

## The Star Runner
Jax's ship, the Star Runner, is a modified YT-1300 freighter known for its speed and unconventional modifications. Despite its appearance, it's one of the fastest ships in the galaxy.

## Role in the War
- Transported Kael Torren and Orin Kestrel across the galaxy
- Participated in the Battle of the Void Core
- Led smuggling operations to supply the Freedom Coalition
- Final assault on Lord Malachar's fortress

## Personality
- Sarcastic and quick-witted
- Initially self-serving, later heroic
- Skilled negotiator and con artist
- Loyal to friends once trust is earned

## Relationships
- **Kael Torren**: Best friend and brother-in-arms
- **Lyra Solari**: Initial tension, later mutual respect
- **Brakk**: Loyal co-pilot and friend
- **Dax Vonn**: Old friend and rival
