# Cryoplan

## Overview
Cryoplan is an ice planet located in the Hoth system. A remote and inhospitable world, it served as the main base of operations for the Freedom Coalition.

## Geography
- Terrain: Frozen wasteland, ice mountains
- Climate: Sub-zero temperatures
- Notable locations: Echo Base

## The Battle of Cryoplan
The Dominion Authority discovered Echo Base through probe droids. Xarn Velgor led an assault with AT-AT walkers.
