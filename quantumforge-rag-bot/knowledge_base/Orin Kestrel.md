# Orin Kestrel

## Overview
Orin Kestrel is a legendary Flux Guardian and mentor to Kael Torren. One of the last surviving members of the Order of Wardens, he lived in exile on Aridus Prime to protect the son of Xarn Velgor.

## Background
Orin was a respected Flux Guardian who fought in the Clone Wars. After the rise of the Dominion Authority and the purge of the Wardens, he went into hiding on Aridus Prime, watching over Kael Torren from afar.

## Role as Mentor
- Trained Kael Torren in the ways of the Flux
- Taught him to control his Flux-sensitivity
- Guided him on his journey to become a Flux Guardian
- Sacrificed himself to save Kael from Xarn Velgor

## Abilities
- Master of the Plasma Blade
- Deep understanding of the Radiant Path
- Ability to become a Flux Echo after death
- Wise counselor and strategist

## Legacy
Orin Kestrel's wisdom and sacrifice inspired a new generation of Flux Wardens. His teachings lived on through Kael Torren and the restored Order.

## Notable Quotes
"The Flux will be with you, always."
"You're going to find that many of the truths we cling to depend greatly on our own point of view."
