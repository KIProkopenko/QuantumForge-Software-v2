# Aridus Prime

## Overview
Aridus Prime is a harsh desert planet located in the Outer Rim Territories. Known for its twin suns, endless dunes, and moisture farming communities, it is the homeworld of Kael Torren.

## Geography
- Terrain: Endless desert with rocky canyons
- Climate: Extremely hot and arid
- Moons: 3 (Tatoo I, Tatoo II, Tatoo III)
- Notable locations: Dusthaven, Obi-Wan's Cave, Great Pit of Nar Shaddaa

## Culture
- Population: Primarily human settlers
- Economy: Moisture farming, scavenging
- Government: Minimal, controlled by local authorities
- Notable species: Humans, Jawas, Sand People

## History
- Settled by refugees centuries ago
- Home to Orin Kestrel during exile
- Raised Kael Torren in hiding
- Site of early skirmishes with Shadow Troopers

## Notable Features
- Twin suns create extreme temperatures
- Sandstorms can last for days
- Jawa traders roam the desert
- Ancient ruins scattered across the surface

## Role in the War
Aridus Prime served as the starting point for Kael Torren's journey. After Shadow Troopers killed his uncle and aunt, Kael left with Orin Kestrel to join the Freedom Coalition.
