# Xarn Velgor

## Overview
Xarn Velgor is the supreme leader of the Dominion Authority and one of the most powerful Void Masters in known history. Once a promising Flux Guardian, he fell to the Void Path and became the enforcer of Lord Malachar's will.

## Background
Originally known as a talented Flux-sensitive warrior, Xarn was seduced by the promise of unlimited power through the Void Path. After a tragic loss, he surrendered to darkness and was reborn as Xarn Velgor, clad in black armor that sustains his damaged form.

## Abilities
- Master of the Plasma Blade
- Profound connection to the Void Path
- Strategic genius and military commander
- Ability to sense Flux disturbances across star systems

## Notable Actions
- Led the assault on the Freedom Coalition headquarters
- Hunted down Flux Wardens across the galaxy
- Commanded the Void Core superweapon
- Final confrontation with his son, Kael Torren

## Relationships
- **Lord Malachar**: Master and Emperor
- **Kael Torren**: Son and eventual redeemer
- **Orin Kestrel**: Former mentor
- **Lyra Solari**: Former ally turned enemy
