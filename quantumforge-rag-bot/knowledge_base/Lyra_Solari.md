# Lyra Solari

## Overview
Lyra Solari is the leader of the Freedom Coalition and a key figure in the resistance against the Dominion Authority. A former royal princess of Alderis Prime.

## Background
Lyra was raised as a princess on Alderis Prime. When the Dominion Authority destroyed her homeworld, she joined the Freedom Coalition and quickly rose to leadership.

## Role in the Resistance
- Organized the theft of the Void Core schematics
- Led the assault on the first Void Core
- Coordinated guerrilla operations across the galaxy

## Skills
- Expert tactician and strategist
- Skilled diplomat and negotiator
- Proficient with blaster pistols
